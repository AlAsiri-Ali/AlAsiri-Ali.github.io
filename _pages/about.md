## Profile

I am **Ali AlAsiri**, an **AI Systems Engineer** from Saudi Arabia. I build and evaluate systems across RAG, computer vision, language, and decision-support workflows with an emphasis on clarity, reliability, and measurable results.

My **Bachelor's degree in Information Systems** from **King Khalid University** shaped how I think about users, data, processes, databases, and organizational workflows. I later completed a **Professional Master in Artificial Intelligence** at **King Abdulaziz University** with an Excellent grade.
